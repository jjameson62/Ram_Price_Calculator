PROGRAM: RAM Price Calculator

PURPOSE:

GROUP MEMBERS:

    Gordon  – Price calculations

INPUTS:

    DDR generation
    Capacity in GB


OUTPUTS:
    Estimated price
    Price per GB

BEGIN PROGRAM

    GET the user's RAM specifications

    CALCULATE the estimated price and price per GB
    DISPLAY the results
END PROGRAM
